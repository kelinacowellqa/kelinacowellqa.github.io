🗺️ Quest & Progression Testing

As development progressed, my testing expanded into focused validation of existing quest content ahead of further planned content development.

I carried out exploratory quest playthroughs from a first-time-player perspective, testing progression flow, objective clarity, inventory interactions, puzzles, resource gathering, crafting, cooking, and NPC dialogue.

A key part of this testing was evaluating not only whether quest systems functioned correctly, but whether the intended progression could be understood using the information available to the player.

Where progression became unclear, I avoided requesting the intended solution from the developer. Instead, I tested logical player actions, documented the resulting behaviour, and reported where the progression path could not be determined naturally. This preserved the value of the first-time-player perspective and helped identify potential discoverability and guidance issues.

The testing included both successful end-to-end quest validation and investigation of progression friction. Individual systems were tested independently where appropriate to help distinguish between a functional problem and unclear communication of the intended interaction.

Dialogue was also reviewed during these passes for grammar, spelling, punctuation, capitalisation, and formatting issues.

⚠️ Current Player-Facing Risk Areas

Risk Area Player Impact
Progression Clarity Players may become unsure how to progress when the intended next action is not sufficiently communicated.
System Discoverability Some systems can be overlooked or misunderstood without sufficient guidance.
Feature Communication Mechanics may function correctly while their purpose or intended use remains unclear.
Quest & System Guidance Players may understand an objective but struggle to identify the required item, interaction, or system needed to progress.
Feedback Visibility Important information can occasionally be easy to miss during normal play.
Retention Risk Repeated uncertainty around progression or system use can interrupt gameplay flow and reduce motivation to continue.
